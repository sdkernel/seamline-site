Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Seamline does not collect any data about you.

Your photos stay on your device

Seamline stitches panoramas entirely on your iPhone or iPad. Photos you import, the panoramas you create and everything Seamline computes from them are stored only on your device, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded, and Seamline makes no network connections.

Photo library access

  • Importing: when you choose photos with the system photo picker, Seamline receives only the photos you select. It never sees the rest of your library.
  • Saving: when you export to your photo library, Seamline asks for permission to add photos. It cannot read your library with this permission.

Photo metadata, such as camera model, lens, capture time and drone orientation, is read on your device only, to align the photos. It is not transmitted anywhere.

No accounts, tracking, analytics or advertising

Seamline has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Crash reports

If you choose to share crash reports or app analytics with developers in your device settings, Apple provides anonymous crash data to us under Apple's privacy policy. We use it only to fix bugs.

Deleting your data

Deleting a project in Seamline removes its photos and results from the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Exports you saved to Photos or Files stay there until you delete them.

Children

Seamline does not collect any information, including from children.
