Seamline
by Kernelfusion Pty Ltd

Gigapixel panoramas from your drone or phone

Seamline turns overlapping photos into seamless panoramas, entirely on your iPhone or iPad, and exports them at full resolution.

  • Fully automatic: rows, capture order, lens and exposure are worked out for you, using your drone's gimbal data.
  • Sub-pixel alignment on the original photos, with exposure and colour matched across every frame.
  • Lossless full-resolution TIFF, and JPEG up to 65,500 pixels wide.
  • Private by design: no account, no uploads, no tracking.
